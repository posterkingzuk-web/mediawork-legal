BONUS NIGHT

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Bonus Night is a party app for logging slot nights with friends. It works offline and has no accounts.

What stays on your phone

  • Player names, avatars, house rules, turns, multipliers, budgets, notes, achievements and history are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • You can delete everything any time in More, under Delete all my data.

What we receive

  • Purchases: handled by Apple or Google through RevenueCat. We see whether Pro is active and an anonymous ID, never card details.
  • Anonymous usage events (if enabled), such as "night started" or "wheel spun", to improve the app. These carry no names, no amounts and no location.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell data, show ads, link to casinos or take bets.
  • We don't collect money amounts. Budget figures stay on your device.

Game data

The game catalogue is from slot.report (CC BY 4.0).

Adults only

Bonus Night is for adults aged 18+ (21+ in the US).
