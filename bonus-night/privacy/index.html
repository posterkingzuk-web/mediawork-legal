BONUS NIGHT

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Bonus Night is a party app for logging slot nights with friends. Solo and pass-the-phone nights work offline with no account. Online parties and friends are optional.

What stays on your phone

  • Player names, house rules, turns, multipliers, budgets, balances, notes, achievements and history for offline nights are stored only on your device.
  • You can delete everything any time in More, under Delete all my data.

Online parties and friends (optional)

If you create a profile to play online or add friends, we store the following with Google Firebase (servers in London, UK):

  • Your player name (gamer tag), avatar, colour and, if you add one, a small profile photo and the casino site name you choose. Other players can see these.
  • An anonymous account ID created by Firebase. No email, phone number or password.
  • Friends and invites: who you have added and party invites you send or receive.
  • Party nights: while you play an online party, that night's players, turns, balances you enter and emoji reactions are shared with everyone in the party.

Delete all my data in More also deletes your online profile, player name and friendships.

What else we receive

  • Purchases: handled by Apple or Google through RevenueCat. We see whether Pro is active and an anonymous ID, never card details.
  • Anonymous usage events (if enabled), such as "night started" or "wheel spun", to improve the app. These carry no names, no amounts and no location.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell data, show ads, link to casinos or take bets.
  • We never connect to your casino account. Balances are only what you type in.

Adults only

Bonus Night is for adults aged 18+ (21+ in the US).
