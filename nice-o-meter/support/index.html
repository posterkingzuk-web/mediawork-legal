Support

How do I set the result?

Open Parent Corner: press and hold the gold star at the top of the home screen, then enter your grown-up PIN. There you can set each child to Nice, Watch List, Naughty, or "Let Santa decide". During a scan you can also tap the left, middle or right of the dial to steer the result.

I forgot my PIN

For safety there's no PIN recovery. Delete and reinstall the app to start again (this removes your children and settings).

Santa is silent

Turn your phone's volume up. Santa's voice and the sounds can be switched on in Parent Corner, under Settings.

Restore the North Pole Pass

Parent Corner, Restore purchases, using the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.

Delete my data

Remove a child in Parent Corner, or uninstall the app. There's no account, so nothing is stored on our side.
