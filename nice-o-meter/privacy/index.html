NICE-O-METER

Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Nice-o-Meter is a pretend "naughty or nice" scanner for Christmas. A grown-up sets up the app and secretly chooses the result; the child sees a fun scan. It works offline with no account.

What stays on your phone

  • Your children's names, ages, photos, the Nice-o-Meter scores and all settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The camera is used during the scan so the child can see themselves on screen, and to take an optional photo for the certificate. Camera images are shown and saved only on your device and are never uploaded.
  • You can remove a child, delete a photo, or uninstall the app at any time to erase everything.

What we receive

  • Purchases: the North Pole Pass is handled by Apple or Google through RevenueCat. We see whether the Pass is active and an anonymous ID, never your name or card details.
  • Anonymous usage events (if enabled), such as "scan finished", to improve the app. These carry no names, no photos and no location.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking across apps, and we don't sell data.
  • Photos and children's details never leave the phone. No location access.

Sharing the certificate

If you tap Share on a Nice List certificate, the app makes an image and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes is up to you.

Children

Nice-o-Meter is designed to be set up and controlled by a parent or guardian. It collects no personal data from anyone and sends nothing about a child off the device. Purchases sit behind a grown-ups-only check.
