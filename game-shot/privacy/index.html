GAME SHOT

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Game Shot is a darts scoring app with a caller voice. It works offline with no account.

What stays on your phone

  • Player names, scores, matches, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The caller uses your phone's own text-to-speech. Nothing you score is sent anywhere to be spoken.
  • You can delete your match history any time in Settings, under Delete all match history. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

What we receive

  • Purchases: handled by Apple or Google through RevenueCat. We see whether Pro is active and an anonymous ID, never your name or card details.
  • Anonymous usage events (if enabled), such as "match finished", to improve the app. These carry no names and no location.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking across apps, and we don't sell data.
  • No microphone, camera or location access.

Sharing results

If you tap Share result, the app makes an image of the result and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes is up to you.

Children

Game Shot is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone.
