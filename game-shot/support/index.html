Support

The caller is silent

Check the speaker icon at the top of the match screen and Settings, Caller voice. Turn your phone's volume up and the silent switch off. On Android, make sure a text-to-speech engine is installed (Settings, Accessibility, Text-to-speech).

Restore Pro

Settings, Restore purchases, using the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.

Delete my data

Settings, Delete all match history. There's no account, so nothing is stored on our side.
